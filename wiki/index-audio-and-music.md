# Audio and Music

- [beep](beep.md) — A simple library for playback and audio manipulation.
- [flac](flac.md) — Native Go FLAC encoder/decoder with support for FLAC streams.
- [gaad](gaad.md) — Native Go AAC bitstream parser.
- [go-aac](go-aac.md) — Pure-Go AAC-LC encoder and decoder ported from FFmpeg.
- [go-audio-resampler](go-audio-resampler.md) — Pure-Go, high-quality audio resampler with SIMD acceleration.
- [go-flac](go-flac.md) — Native Go FLAC encoder and decoder with SIMD acceleration.
- [go-mpris](go-mpris.md) — Client for mpris dbus interfaces.
- [go-opus](go-opus.md) — Native Go implementation of the Opus audio codec (RFC 6716) with RFC-compliant decoder.
- [go-resample](go-resample.md) — Pure-Go (no cgo) audio sample-rate converter with sinc, linear, and zero-order-hold converters.
- [go-wav](go-wav.md) — Pure-Go WAV/RIFF reader and writer with RF64 and BW64 support for files larger than 4 GiB.
- [GoAudio](goaudio.md) — Native Go Audio Processing Library.
- [gocue](gocue.md) — Audio analysis CLI that detects cue-in, cue-out, and overlay points and measures EBU R128 loudness, emitting JSON for Liquidsoap.
- [gosamplerate](gosamplerate.md) — libsamplerate bindings for go.
- [id3v2](id3v2.md) — ID3 decoding and encoding library for Go.
- [malgo](malgo.md) — Mini audio library.
- [minimp3](minimp3.md) — Lightweight MP3 decoder library.
- [music-theory](music-theory.md) — Music theory models in Go.
- [Oto](oto.md) — A low-level library to play sound on multiple platforms.
- [PortAudio](portaudio.md) — Go bindings for the PortAudio audio I/O library.
- [voxrai-ai](voxrai-ai.md) — AI voice agents with a JSON configuration, STT → LLM → TTS pipelines over WebSocket and WebRTC.
