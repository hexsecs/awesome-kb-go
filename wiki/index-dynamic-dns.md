# Dynamic DNS

- [DDNS](ddns.md) — Personal DDNS client with Digital Ocean Networking DNS as backend.
- [dyndns](dyndns.md) — Background Go process to regularly and automatically check your IP Address and make updates to (one or many) Dynamic DNS records for Google domains whenever…
- [GoDNS](godns.md) — A dynamic DNS client tool, supports DNSPod & HE.net, written in Go.
